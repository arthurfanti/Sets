# 07: History

**What to build:** Seeing what you actually did. Finished Sessions listed newest-first under month headers, expandable in place to their Sets, correctable, and deletable.

**Blocked by:** 06

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] History lists finished Sessions newest-first, grouped under month headers.
- [ ] Each row names the Routine (or "Sessão livre") and shows date, duration and Set count.
- [ ] Tapping a row expands it in place to list that Session's Sets; no separate screen is introduced.
- [ ] A Set's weight and reps can be corrected, and the correction survives a reload.
- [ ] A finished Session can be deleted behind a confirmation naming its Routine and date, and it disappears from volume afterwards.
- [ ] A deleted Exercise still renders its name inside an expanded Session rather than showing a blank.
- [ ] The expanded row is reachable and dismissible by keyboard and assistive technology, with focus returning to the row that was expanded.
